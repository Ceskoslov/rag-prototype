# Data Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliance for Passenger Data)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our passengers, employees, and partners. This policy outlines SkyWing's data privacy principles and practices, specifically addressing compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable privacy laws.

## Scope
This policy applies to all personal data collected, processed, and stored by SkyWing Airlines, including:
- **Passenger Data**: Name, address, contact details, passport/ID details, payment information (credit card numbers), travel history, and loyalty program (SkyWing Rewards) information.
- **Employee Data**: Name, contact details, payroll information, performance reviews, and health-related information (if required for travel or leave).
- **Partner and Vendor Data**: Contact details and business-related information.

## Core Data Privacy Principles (GDPR)
SkyWing adheres to the following GDPR principles for the processing of personal data:
- **Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency**: Personal data will be processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data will be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes (e.g., flight booking, loyalty program management).
- **Data Minimization**: Personal data collected will be adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the purposes for which it is processed.
- **Accuracy**: Personal data will be accurate and, where necessary, kept up to date.
- **Storage Limitation**: Data will be kept in a form which permits identification of data subjects for no longer than is necessary.
- **Integrity and Confidentiality**: Data will be processed in a manner that ensures appropriate security, including protection against unauthorized or unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction, or damage.

## Lawful Basis for Processing
SkyWing processes personal data based on one of the following legal grounds:
- **Contractual Necessity**: Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract (e.g., to process a flight booking).
- **Legal Obligation**: Processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements (e.g., reporting passenger manifests to immigration authorities).
- **Legitimate Interests**: Processing is necessary for SkyWing's legitimate business interests (e.g., fraud prevention, marketing communications - where permitted).
- **Consent**: Processing is based on the data subject's explicit consent (e.g., for certain types of personalized marketing or sensitive data processing).

## Data Subject Rights
Under the GDPR, passengers and employees have the following rights regarding their personal data:
- **Right of Access**: The right to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data is being processed and to access that data.
- **Right to Rectification**: The right to have inaccurate personal data corrected.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")**: The right to have personal data erased in certain circumstances (e.g., if it is no longer necessary for the original purpose).
- **Right to Restriction of Processing**: The right to restrict the processing of personal data in specific cases.
- **Right to Data Portability**: The right to receive personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
- **Right to Object**: The right to object to the processing of personal data (e.g., for direct marketing).

## Data Subject Access Requests (DSARs)
Any person wishing to exercise their rights under the GDPR may submit a Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) to the SkyWing Data Protection Officer (DPO) via the online portal at `privacy.skywing.com/request`.
- **Response Time**: SkyWing will respond to DSARs within one month of receipt. This period may be extended by an additional two months for complex or numerous requests.
- **Verification**: SkyWing will verify the identity of the requester before disclosing any personal data.

## Data Sharing and Transfers
- **Third-Party Service Providers**: SkyWing may share personal data with trusted third-party service providers (e.g., ground handling agents, IT service providers, payment processors) who process data on our behalf.
- **International Transfers**: Personal data may be transferred to countries outside the EEA. SkyWing ensures that such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or adequacy decisions by the European Commission.

## Data Security Measures
SkyWing implements robust technical and organizational measures to protect personal data, including:
- Encryption of data in transit and at rest.
- Access controls and multi-factor authentication.
- Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
- Employee training on data privacy and security best practices.

## Data Retention Policy
- **Passenger Bookings**: Retained for 7 years following the completion of the travel to comply with tax and legal requirements.
- **Loyalty Program Data**: Retained for as long as the member remains active in the SkyWing Rewards program plus 3 years.
- **Marketing Data**: Retained until the data subject withdraws their consent or objects to the processing.

Any suspected or actual data breach must be reported immediately to the Security Operations Center (SOC) and the DPO.
- **SOC Hotline**: Extension 9111
- **DPO Email**: `privacy-breach@skywing.com`
- **Notification Period**: In accordance with the GDPR, SkyWing will notify the relevant supervisory authority of a high-risk data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
