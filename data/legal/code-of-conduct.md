# Employee Code of Conduct

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to maintaining the highest standards of ethics, integrity, and professionalism in all its operations. This Employee Code of Conduct (Code) outlines the expected behavior and responsibilities for all SkyWing employees, contractors, and representatives.

## Core Values
SkyWing's core values are:
- **Safety**: Putting safety first in everything we do.
- **Integrity**: Acting with honesty and ethical principles.
- **Respect**: Treating others with dignity and fairness.
- **Professionalism**: Maintaining the highest standards of professionalism in our work.
- **Service**: Providing excellent service to our passengers and each other.

## Compliance with Laws and Regulations
All SkyWing employees must comply with all applicable laws and regulations in the jurisdictions in which SkyWing operates, including:
- **Aviation Laws and Regulations** (e.g., FAA, EASA).
- **Labor and Employment Laws**.
- **Data Privacy Laws** (e.g., GDPR).
- **Anti-Corruption and Anti-Bribery Laws**.
- **Environmental Regulations**.

## Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid any situation that could lead to a conflict of interest or even the appearance of a conflict of interest. A conflict of interest arises when an employee's personal interests interfere with their ability to act in the best interests of SkyWing.
- **Gifts and Entertainment**: Employees must not accept gifts, entertainment, or other favors from vendors, partners, or competitors that could influence their business decisions.
- **Outside Employment**: Employees must not engage in any outside employment or business activity that competes with SkyWing or interferes with their job performance.
- **Financial Interests**: Employees must disclose any financial interest they or their immediate family members have in any vendor, partner, or competitor of SkyWing.

## Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
- **Confidential Information**: Employees must protect and maintain the confidentiality of SkyWing's proprietary information, including passenger data, financial records, business strategies, and trade secrets.
- **Intellectual Property**: All intellectual property created by employees during the course of their employment with SkyWing is the property of SkyWing. Employees must respect the intellectual property rights of others.

## Equal Opportunity and Non-Discrimination
SkyWing is an equal opportunity employer and is committed to providing a work environment that is free from discrimination and harassment.
- **Non-Discrimination**: SkyWing does not discriminate against any employee or applicant based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, or any other characteristic protected by law.
- **Non-Harassment**: SkyWing does not tolerate any form of harassment, including sexual harassment, bullying, or verbal abuse.

## Use of Corporate Assets
Employees must use SkyWing's corporate assets (e.g., computers, software, email, vehicles, facilities) for legitimate business purposes only and in accordance with SkyWing's policies.
- **Information Technology**: Employees must comply with SkyWing's IT security policies and must not use corporate IT resources for illegal or inappropriate activities.
- **Financial Resources**: Employees must handle SkyWing's financial resources with care and in accordance with approved budgets and financial procedures.

## Reporting Misconduct
SkyWing encourages employees to report any suspected or actual misconduct or violation of this Code.
- **SkyWing Ethics Hotline**: Reports can be made anonymously via the SkyWing Ethics Hotline at `1-800-SKYWING-ETHICS` or online at `ethics.skywing.com`.
- **Whistleblower Protection**: SkyWing prohibits retaliation against any employee who in good faith reports a violation of this Code or participates in an investigation.

## Disciplinary Action
Failure to comply with this Code or other SkyWing policies may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal action.
