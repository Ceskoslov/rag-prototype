# Intellectual Property and Confidentiality Agreements

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to protecting its intellectual property and maintaining the confidentiality of its proprietary information. This document outlines the intellectual property and confidentiality agreements that apply to all SkyWing employees, contractors, and partners.

## Intellectual Property Rights
Intellectual property includes any creative works, inventions, designs, or other proprietary information developed or created by SkyWing employees or contractors during the course of their employment or engagement with SkyWing.
- **Ownership**: All intellectual property created by employees or contractors during their work with SkyWing is the exclusive property of SkyWing. This includes, but is not limited to, software code, research, designs, trademarks, and trade secrets.
- **Invention Assignment**: All employees and contractors must sign an Invention Assignment Agreement, which assigns to SkyWing all rights, titles, and interests in any inventions or other intellectual property created by them during their work with SkyWing.
- **Copyrights**: SkyWing owns the copyright in any creative works created by employees or contractors as part of their job responsibilities. This includes documents, reports, presentations, and other materials.

## Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure
Confidentiality is critical to SkyWing's success and reputation. All employees, contractors, and partners must maintain the confidentiality of SkyWing's proprietary information.
- **Confidential Information**: Confidential information includes, but is not limited to:
    - Passenger data and personal information.
    - Financial records and business strategies.
    - Marketing plans and customer lists.
    - Trade secrets and proprietary technology.
    - Any information marked as "Confidential" or "Proprietary."
- **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)**: All employees, contractors, and partners must sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) as a condition of their employment or engagement with SkyWing. The NDA prohibits the disclosure of SkyWing's confidential information to any third party without SkyWing's prior written consent.
- **Duration**: The obligation of confidentiality continues even after an employee's employment or a contractor's engagement with SkyWing has ended.

## Use of SkyWing's Intellectual Property
Employees and contractors may use SkyWing's intellectual property only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with SkyWing's policies.
- **Trademarks**: SkyWing's trademarks (e.g., SkyWing logo, "SkyWing Rewards") must be used only in accordance with SkyWing's brand guidelines.
- **Software**: Software developed or licensed by SkyWing must be used only for SkyWing business and in accordance with the relevant license agreements.

## Protection of Third-Party Intellectual Property
SkyWing respects the intellectual property rights of others.
- **Software Licensing**: Employees and contractors must use only software that has been properly licensed by SkyWing and must comply with the terms of the license agreements.
- **Confidential Information of Others**: Employees and contractors must not use or disclose any confidential information of a third party that they have obtained through their work with SkyWing, unless they have the third party's written consent.

## Reporting Violations
Employees and contractors must report any suspected or actual violation of this document or SkyWing's intellectual property and confidentiality policies.
- **SkyWing Legal Department**: Reports can be made to the Legal Department at `legal-ip@skywing.com`.
- **SkyWing Ethics Hotline**: Reports can also be made via the SkyWing Ethics Hotline at `1-800-SKYWING-ETHICS`.

## Disciplinary Action and Legal Remedies
Failure to comply with this document or SkyWing's intellectual property and confidentiality policies may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or engagement. SkyWing also reserves the right to seek legal remedies, including injunctive relief and damages, for any violation of its intellectual property rights or confidentiality agreements.
