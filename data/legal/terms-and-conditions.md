# Terms and Conditions of Carriage

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is a fictional airline. These Terms and Conditions of Carriage (T&C) apply to all flights operated by SkyWing or on SkyWing's behalf, including codeshare flights operated by partner airlines. By booking a flight with SkyWing, passengers agree to be bound by these T&C.

## Contract of Carriage
The contract of carriage between SkyWing and the passenger consists of the passenger's ticket (electronic or paper), the conditions of contract, these T&C, and any other relevant airline regulations.

## Passenger Booking and Identification
- **Accurate Information**: Passengers must provide accurate and complete information at the time of booking, including their full name as it appears on their passport/ID, contact details, and any special assistance requirements.
- **Identification**: All passengers must present valid government-issued identification at check-in and boarding. For international flights, a valid passport and, if necessary, a visa or other travel documents are required.
- **Name Changes**: Name changes are generally not permitted after a booking is confirmed. If a name change is required, it may be subject to a fee or require a new booking.

## Fares and Taxes
- **Fares**: Fares are subject to availability and may change without notice. The fare paid at the time of booking is the fare that applies to the ticket.
- **Taxes and Fees**: Fares include applicable taxes, fees, and surcharges. Passengers are responsible for paying any additional taxes or fees that may be imposed by government authorities or airport operators.
- **Non-Refundable Fares**: Certain fares are non-refundable. In the event of a cancellation by the passenger, the fare may be forfeited, and only certain taxes and fees may be eligible for a refund.

## Baggage
- **Free Baggage Allowance**: Passengers are entitled to a free baggage allowance, which varies depending on the fare class and destination. Detailed information on baggage allowances is available on the SkyWing website.
- **Excess Baggage**: Baggage that exceeds the free allowance will be subject to excess baggage fees.
- **Prohibited Items**: For safety and security reasons, certain items are prohibited in checked and/or carry-on baggage. These include explosives, flammable liquids, and toxic substances. A full list of prohibited items is available at airport check-in counters.
- **Baggage Liability**: SkyWing's liability for lost, damaged, or delayed baggage is limited in accordance with the Montreal Convention and other applicable laws.

## Check-In and Boarding
- **Check-In Deadlines**: Passengers must complete check-in at least 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time for domestic flights and 90 minutes for international flights.
- **Boarding Deadlines**: Passengers must be at the boarding gate at least 30 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
- **Failure to Comply**: SkyWing reserves the right to cancel a passenger's reservation or deny boarding if they fail to comply with check-in and boarding deadlines.

## Flight Disruptions
- **Schedules**: SkyWing will use its best efforts to carry passengers and their baggage in accordance with the published schedule. However, schedules are not guaranteed and do not form part of the contract of carriage.
- **Cancellations and Delays**: In the event of a flight cancellation or significant delay, SkyWing will provide assistance and compensation in accordance with its Passenger Rights and Compensation Policy and applicable laws (e.g., EU261).

## Refusal of Carriage
SkyWing reserves the right to refuse carriage or remove a passenger from a flight for reasons of safety, security, or public order, including if:
- The passenger's conduct is disruptive, abusive, or endangers the safety of the aircraft or other passengers.
- The passenger is under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- The passenger fails to comply with the instructions of the crew.
- The passenger does not have the necessary travel documents.

## Governing Law and Jurisdiction
These T&C and the contract of carriage are governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the ticket was issued, and any disputes will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of that jurisdiction.

## Modifications
SkyWing reserves the right to modify these T&C at any time. The T&C in effect at the time of booking will apply to the contract of carriage.
