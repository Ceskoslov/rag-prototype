# Baggage Liability and Claims Procedures

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to handling passenger baggage with care and in accordance with international and regional regulations. This document outlines SkyWing's liability for lost, damaged, and delayed baggage and the procedures for submitting baggage claims.

## International Liability (Montreal Convention)
SkyWing's liability for lost, damaged, or delayed baggage on international flights is governed by the Montreal Convention 1999 (MC99).
- **Liability Limit**: Under MC99, SkyWing's liability for baggage is limited to approximately 1,288 Special Drawing Rights (SDR) per passenger (approximately $1,700 USD).
- **Scope**: This limit applies to all checked and unchecked baggage on international flights, unless the passenger has made a "Special Declaration of Interest" at check-in and paid an additional fee.
- **Exceptions**: SkyWing is not liable for damage resulting from inherent defect, quality, or vice of the baggage, or for baggage that was already damaged at the time of check-in.

## Domestic Liability (US DOT)
For domestic flights within the United States, SkyWing's liability for baggage is governed by the Department of Transportation (DOT) regulations.
- **Liability Limit**: The current minimum liability limit for domestic baggage is $3,800 USD per passenger.
- **Scope**: This limit applies to checked baggage on domestic flights. SkyWing is not liable for fragile, perishable, or valuable items (e.g., jewelry, electronics, cash) in checked baggage.

## Damaged Baggage
If your checked baggage is damaged, you must report the damage to a SkyWing representative at the airport before leaving the baggage claim area.
- **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)**: A PIR must be completed and a copy provided to the passenger.
- **Claim Submission**: For damaged baggage, a written claim must be submitted to SkyWing within 7 days of receiving the baggage.
- **Inspection**: SkyWing may request to inspect the damaged baggage and its contents before approving a claim.

## Delayed Baggage
If your checked baggage does not arrive on your flight, you must report it to a SkyWing representative at the airport before leaving the baggage claim area.
- **Property Irregularity Report (PIR)**: A PIR must be completed and a copy provided to the passenger.
- **SkyWing Assistance**: SkyWing will use its best efforts to locate and deliver the delayed baggage to the passenger's local address.
- **Reasonable Expenses**: For international flights, SkyWing will reimburse passengers for reasonable expenses incurred for necessary items (e.g., toiletries, undergarments) while their baggage is delayed, subject to receipts.
- **Claim Submission**: For delayed baggage, a written claim for expenses must be submitted to SkyWing within 21 days of receiving the baggage.

## Lost Baggage
Checked baggage is considered "lost" if it has not been located within 21 days of the scheduled arrival date.
- **Claim Submission**: For lost baggage, a written claim for the value of the baggage and its contents must be submitted to SkyWing within 30 days of the baggage being declared lost.
- **Valuation**: Passengers must provide receipts or other proof of value for items in their lost baggage. SkyWing's liability is limited to the maximum amounts specified by MC99 or DOT regulations.

## Submitting a Claim
Baggage claims must be submitted in writing via the SkyWing online baggage claims portal at `legal.skywing.com/baggage-claims`.
- **Required Information**:
    - Passenger name and contact information.
    - Booking reference (PNR).
    - Property Irregularity Report (PIR) number.
    - Description of the baggage and its contents.
    - Receipts or other proof of value for damaged or lost items.
- **Response Time**: SkyWing will respond to all baggage claims within 30 days of receipt.

## Exclusions from Liability
SkyWing is not liable for:
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., minor scratches, scuffs, or dents).
- Damage caused by over-packing.
- Fragile or perishable items in checked baggage.
- Valuable items (e.g., jewelry, electronics, cash, important documents) in checked baggage. Passengers are encouraged to carry such items in their carry-on baggage.
