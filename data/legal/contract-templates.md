# Contract Templates for Vendor Agreements

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) uses standardized contract templates for its vendor agreements to ensure consistency, clarity, and protection of its interests. This document provides an overview of the key contract templates used by SkyWing for various types of vendor agreements.

## Standard Master Services Agreement (MSA)
The Master Services Agreement (MSA) is the primary contract template for all vendor agreements. It sets out the general terms and conditions that apply to all services provided by the vendor.
- **Scope of Services**: The MSA defines the services to be provided by the vendor, which are typically specified in a Statement of Work (SOW).
- **Fees and Payment**: The MSA specifies the fees to be paid for the services and the payment terms.
- **Intellectual Property Rights**: The MSA defines the ownership and use of any intellectual property created during the performance of the services.
- **Confidentiality and Non-Disclosure**: The MSA includes a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) to protect SkyWing's confidential information.
- **Indemnification and Liability**: The MSA specifies the vendor's indemnification obligations and the limitations on liability for both parties.
- **Term and Termination**: The MSA defines the term of the agreement and the conditions under which it can be terminated.
- **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**: The MSA specifies the law that governs the agreement and the jurisdiction in which disputes will be resolved.

## Statement of Work (SOW)
The Statement of Work (SOW) is a subsidiary document that defines the specific tasks, deliverables, and timelines for a particular project or engagement.
- **Project Description**: The SOW provides a detailed description of the project and the services to be provided.
- **Deliverables**: The SOW specifies the deliverables to be produced by the vendor and the acceptance criteria for each deliverable.
- **Timeline and Milestones**: The SOW defines the project timeline and the key milestones to be achieved.
- **Fees and Payment**: The SOW specifies the fees for the project and the payment schedule.

## Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)
The Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is a standalone contract template used to protect SkyWing's confidential information when shared with a third party before an MSA is in place.
- **Definition of Confidential Information**: The NDA defines what information is considered confidential and the obligations of the receiving party to protect it.
- **Exclusions**: The NDA specifies the information that is not considered confidential (e.g., information that is already in the public domain).
- **Term and Termination**: The NDA defines the term of the agreement and the conditions under which it can be terminated.
- **Governing Law and Jurisdiction**: The NDA specifies the law that governs the agreement and the jurisdiction in which disputes will be resolved.

## Service Level Agreement (SLA)
The Service Level Agreement (SLA) is an optional contract template used to define the performance standards and service levels for a particular service.
- **Performance Metrics**: The SLA specifies the metrics to be used to measure the vendor's performance (e.g., uptime, response time, resolution time).
- **Service Levels**: The SLA defines the target service levels for each metric.
- **Remedies and Penalties**: The SLA specifies the remedies and penalties for the vendor's failure to meet the target service levels (e.g., service credits).

## Compliance and Security Exhibit
The Compliance and Security Exhibit is an optional contract template used to specify the vendor's compliance and security obligations.
- **Regulatory Compliance**: The exhibit specifies the vendor's obligation to comply with applicable laws and regulations (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
- **Security Standards**: The exhibit defines the security standards and practices the vendor must follow (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2).
- **Audit Rights**: The exhibit grants SkyWing the right to audit the vendor's compliance and security practices.

## Modifications and Customization
All contract templates are subject to modification and customization by SkyWing's Legal Department.
- **Legal Review**: Any significant modifications to the contract templates must be reviewed and approved by the Legal Department.
- **Vendor Negotiation**: SkyWing's Legal Department will negotiate the terms of the vendor agreement with the vendor's legal counsel.
