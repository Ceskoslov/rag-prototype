# Annual Fee Waiver Conditions

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the annual fee waiver conditions for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## Annual Fee Waiver for the First Year
The annual fee for the SkyWing Explorer Card is waived for the first year. The annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card is $95.
- **Waiver Period**: The annual fee for the SkyWing Explorer Card is waived for the first 12 months from the date the card is issued.
- **Waiver Amount**: The $95 annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card is not waived for the first year.

## Annual Fee Waiver for SkyWing Rewards Members
The annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card may be waived for SkyWing Rewards members who meet certain status or spending requirements.
- **SkyWing Rewards Status**: The annual fee is waived for SkyWing Rewards Gold, Platinum, and Diamond members.
- **Status Verification**: SkyWing Rewards status will be verified annually on the card's anniversary date.
- **Status Changes**: If a member's status changes from Gold, Platinum, or Diamond to Silver, the annual fee will be charged on the next anniversary date.

## Annual Fee Waiver for High Spending Cardholders
The annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card may also be waived for cardholders who spend a certain amount on their card in a calendar year.
- **Spending Threshold**: The annual fee is waived for cardholders who spend at least $30,000 on their SkyWing Premier Card in the preceding calendar year.
- **Spending Calculation**: Spending is calculated as the total amount of all net purchases (purchases minus returns and credits) made on the card in a calendar year.
- **Spending Verification**: Spending will be verified annually on the card's anniversary date.

## Other Annual Fee Waiver Conditions
The annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card may be waived in other circumstances at Horizon Bank's discretion.
- **New Account Incentives**: Horizon Bank may offer limited-time annual fee waivers as part of its new account incentives.
- **Customer Retention Programs**: Horizon Bank may offer annual fee waivers as part of its customer retention programs for high-value cardholders.

## Annual Fee Payment and Billing
If the annual fee is not waived, it will be charged to the cardholder's account on the card's anniversary date each year.
- **Billing Statement**: The annual fee will appear on the cardholder's billing statement for the month in which the anniversary date occurs.
- **Payment Terms**: The annual fee is due and payable on the same date as the rest of the balance on the billing statement.
