# Statement Credit and Cashback Policies

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the statement credit and cashback policies for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## Annual Travel Statement Credit
SkyWing Visa Signature Premier cardholders are eligible for an annual travel statement credit of up to $100 USD.
- **Statement Credit Availability**: The annual travel statement credit is available once per calendar year for each cardholder account.
- **Qualified Travel Purchases**: Qualified travel purchases include SkyWing tickets, seat upgrades, baggage fees, and in-flight purchases made through the SkyWing website or mobile app.
- **Statement Credit Calculation**: The statement credit will be applied to the cardholder's account within 30 days of the qualifying purchase.
- **Maximum Credit Amount**: The maximum statement credit amount is $100 USD per calendar year.

## Dining and Grocery Cashback
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders can earn cashback on their dining and grocery purchases.
- **Dining Cashback**: Cardholders earn 1% cashback on all dining purchases made at participating restaurants and cafes.
- **Grocery Cashback**: Cardholders earn 1% cashback on all grocery purchases made at participating supermarkets and grocery stores.
- **Cashback Calculation**: Cashback is calculated based on the total amount of the purchase, including taxes and fees.
- **Cashback Redemption**: Cashback will be credited to the cardholder's account on their monthly billing statement.

## Global Entry and TSA PreCheck Statement Credit
SkyWing Visa Signature Premier cardholders are eligible for a statement credit of up to $100 USD for their Global Entry or TSA PreCheck application fee.
- **Statement Credit Availability**: The Global Entry and TSA PreCheck statement credit is available once every four years for each cardholder account.
- **Application Fee Verification**: The statement credit will be applied to the cardholder's account within 30 days of the qualifying application fee being charged to the card.
- **Maximum Credit Amount**: The maximum statement credit amount is $100 USD.

## In-Flight Purchase Statement Credit
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders are eligible for a 20% statement credit on all in-flight purchases made on SkyWing flights.
- **In-Flight Purchases**: In-flight purchases include food, beverages, and Wi-Fi access purchased on board the aircraft.
- **Statement Credit Calculation**: The 20% statement credit will be applied to the cardholder's account within 30 days of the qualifying purchase.
- **Exclusions**: The in-flight purchase statement credit is not available for duty-free purchases or charity donations.

## Statement Credit and Cashback Verification
Cardholders can view their statement credits and cashback on their SkyWing Rewards account page and on their monthly billing statement.
- **Billing Statement**: All statement credits and cashback will appear on the cardholder's monthly billing statement.
- **Status Verification**: Cardholders can verify their status and eligibility for statement credits and cashback through the Horizon Bank online portal.
