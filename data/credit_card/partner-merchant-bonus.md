# Partner Merchant Bonus Categories

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with various merchant partners to offer SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders additional "SkyWing Miles" on their everyday purchases. This document provides an overview of the partner merchant bonus categories for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## Dining and Grocery Merchants
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders earn 1.5 miles for every $1 spent on dining and groceries.
- **Dining Merchants**: This category includes restaurants, cafes, bars, and food delivery services (e.g., DoorDash, Uber Eats).
- **Grocery Merchants**: This category includes supermarkets, grocery stores, and online grocery delivery services (e.g., Instacart, Amazon Fresh).
- **Bonus Miles Calculation**: Bonus miles are calculated based on the total amount of the purchase, including taxes and fees.
- **Exclusions**: Bonus miles are not earned on purchases made at wholesale clubs (e.g., Costco, Sam's Club) or convenience stores.

## Travel and Transportation Merchants
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders earn 2 miles for every $1 spent on travel and transportation.
- **SkyWing Purchases**: This category includes all SkyWing tickets and services purchased through the SkyWing website or mobile app.
- **Travel Merchants**: This category includes other airlines, hotels, car rental agencies, and travel booking services (e.g., Expedia, Booking.com).
- **Transportation Merchants**: This category includes rideshare services (e.g., Uber, Lyft), public transportation (e.g., trains, buses), and parking fees.
- **Bonus Miles Calculation**: Bonus miles are calculated based on the total amount of the purchase, including taxes and fees.

## Retail and Service Merchants
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders earn 1 mile for every $1 spent on retail and service purchases.
- **Retail Merchants**: This category includes clothing stores, electronics stores, home improvement stores, and department stores.
- **Service Merchants**: This category includes utility payments, insurance premiums, and professional services (e.g., legal, accounting).
- **Bonus Miles Calculation**: Bonus miles are calculated based on the total amount of the purchase, including taxes and fees.

## Limited-Time Bonus Categories
Horizon Bank, the issuer of the SkyWing Visa Signature card, may offer limited-time bonus categories from time to time.
- **Bonus Category Notification**: Cardholders will be notified of limited-time bonus categories via email, text message, or on their billing statement.
- **Bonus Miles Calculation**: Bonus miles for limited-time categories will be calculated in accordance with the terms and conditions of the offer.
- **Exclusions**: Limited-time bonus categories may have certain exclusions and limitations.

## Earning and Redeeming Bonus Miles
Bonus miles earned through partner merchant categories will be added to the cardholder's SkyWing Rewards account within 30 days of the transaction.
- **Miles Verification**: Cardholders can view their bonus miles on their SkyWing Rewards account page and on their billing statement.
- **Miles Redemption**: Bonus miles can be redeemed for flights, seat upgrades, hotel stays, and car rentals in the same way as other SkyWing Miles.
