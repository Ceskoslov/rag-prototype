# Fraud Detection and Dispute Resolution

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the fraud detection and dispute resolution services provided by Horizon Bank for SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders.

## Fraud Detection and Prevention
Horizon Bank uses advanced technology and manual reviews to monitor all transactions on SkyWing Visa Signature cards for signs of fraudulent activity.
- **Real-Time Monitoring**: Horizon Bank's fraud detection system monitors transactions in real-time and identifies those that are out of pattern or consistent with known fraud tactics.
- **Alert System**: If a suspicious transaction is identified, Horizon Bank will send an alert to the cardholder via text message, email, or phone call.
- **Temporary Card Block**: Horizon Bank may temporarily block the cardholder's account while it investigates the suspicious transaction.
- **Customer Verification**: Horizon Bank will contact the cardholder to verify the transaction before unblocking the account.

## Reporting Fraudulent Activity
Cardholders should report any suspected or actual fraudulent activity on their SkyWing Visa Signature card as soon as possible.
- **Horizon Bank Fraud Hotline**: Call `1-800-HORIZON-BANK-FRAUD` (available 24/7).
- **Online Fraud Report**: Submit a fraud report through the Horizon Bank online portal at `horizonbank.com/fraud-report`.
- **Cardholder Responsibility**: Cardholders are responsible for notifying Horizon Bank immediately if their card is lost, stolen, or used without their authorization.

## Dispute Resolution Process
Cardholders can dispute unauthorized or incorrect transactions on their SkyWing Visa Signature card through Horizon Bank's dispute resolution process.
- **Dispute Submission**: Cardholders must submit a dispute in writing via the Horizon Bank online portal or by calling the customer service number on the back of their card.
- **Dispute Period**: Disputes must be submitted within 60 days of the date the transaction appeared on the cardholder's billing statement.
- **Investigation**: Horizon Bank will investigate the dispute and may request additional information or documentation from the cardholder.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Horizon Bank will notify the cardholder of its decision on the dispute within 90 days of receipt of the dispute.
- **Credits and Refunds**: If the dispute is resolved in the cardholder's favor, Horizon Bank will credit the cardholder's account for the amount of the disputed transaction and any associated interest and fees.

## Zero Liability Protection
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders are protected by Visa's Zero Liability Policy, which means they are not held responsible for unauthorized transactions made on their card if they report them to Horizon Bank in a timely manner.
- **Unauthorized Transactions**: Unauthorized transactions are those made without the cardholder's consent and for which the cardholder received no benefit.
- **Timely Notification**: Cardholders must notify Horizon Bank within 60 days of the date the unauthorized transaction appeared on their billing statement to be eligible for Zero Liability Protection.

## Protecting Your Credit Card Information
Cardholders can help prevent fraud by taking the following steps to protect their SkyWing Visa Signature card information:
- **Keep Your Card Secure**: Never leave your card unattended or share your PIN with anyone.
- **Be Careful with Online Transactions**: Only use your card on secure and trusted websites.
- **Monitor Your Billing Statements**: Review your billing statements regularly for any unauthorized or incorrect transactions.
- **Sign Up for Alerts**: Sign up for transaction alerts through the Horizon Bank online portal.
