# Card Application and Approval Criteria

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the card application and approval criteria for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## SkyWing Visa Signature Card Application Process
Customers can apply for the SkyWing Visa Signature card online at `skywing.com/credit-card` or at any SkyWing check-in counter.
- **Online Application**: Customers can complete an online application and receive an instant decision on their application.
- **Airport Application**: Customers can complete a paper application at any SkyWing check-in counter and submit it to a SkyWing representative.
- **Phone Application**: Customers can also apply for the card over the phone by calling Horizon Bank's application hotline at `1-800-HORIZON-BANK-APP`.

## Eligibility Criteria
Applicants for the SkyWing Visa Signature card must meet the following eligibility criteria:
- **Age**: Applicants must be at least 18 years old (21 years old in some states).
- **Identity Verification**: Applicants must provide a valid government-issued ID (e.g., driver's license, passport) and a valid Social Security number or Taxpayer Identification Number.
- **Residency**: Applicants must be a resident of the United States.
- **Income**: Applicants must have a steady source of income.

## Creditworthiness and Approval Criteria
Approval for the SkyWing Visa Signature card is subject to creditworthiness and other factors. Horizon Bank will review the applicant's credit history and other information to determine their eligibility for the card.
- **Credit Score**: Horizon Bank will review the applicant's credit score from one or more major credit bureaus (e.g., Equifax, Experian, TransUnion). A minimum credit score of 670 is generally required for the SkyWing Explorer Card and 720 for the SkyWing Premier Card.
- **Credit History**: Horizon Bank will also review the applicant's credit history, including their payment history, credit utilization, and the length of their credit history.
- **Income and Debt**: Horizon Bank will consider the applicant's income and existing debt to determine their ability to repay the credit card balance.
- **SkyWing Rewards Status**: Horizon Bank may also consider the applicant's SkyWing Rewards status when reviewing their application.

## Instant Decision and Approval
Applicants who apply for the SkyWing Visa Signature card online or over the phone may receive an instant decision on their application.
- **Approved**: If the application is approved, the cardholder will receive their new SkyWing Visa Signature card in the mail within 7-10 business days.
- **Denied**: If the application is denied, Horizon Bank will send a notice to the applicant explaining the reasons for the denial.
- **Pending**: In some cases, Horizon Bank may need additional time to review the application and will notify the applicant of its decision within 30 days.

## Credit Limits
Horizon Bank will determine the credit limit for the cardholder's account based on their creditworthiness and other factors.
- **SkyWing Explorer Card Credit Limits**: Credit limits for the SkyWing Explorer Card typically range from $1,000 to $10,000.
- **SkyWing Premier Card Credit Limits**: Credit limits for the SkyWing Premier Card typically range from $5,000 to $50,000.

## Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
The annual percentage rate (APR) for the SkyWing Visa Signature card is based on the cardholder's creditworthiness and the prevailing market rates.
- **Variable APR**: The APR is a variable rate that is based on the Prime Rate.
- **APR Ranges**: APRs for the SkyWing Visa Signature card typically range from 15.99% to 24.99%.
