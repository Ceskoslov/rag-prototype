# Miles Earning and Redemption Rates

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the miles earning and redemption rates for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## Miles Earning Rates
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders earn "SkyWing Miles" on all purchases.
- **SkyWing Purchases**: Earn 2 miles for every $1 spent on SkyWing tickets and services.
- **Dining and Grocery Purchases**: Earn 1.5 miles for every $1 spent on dining and groceries.
- **All Other Purchases**: Earn 1 mile for every $1 spent on all other purchases.
- **SkyWing Miles Redemption**: SkyWing Miles can be redeemed for flights, seat upgrades, hotel stays, and car rentals.

## Miles Redemption Rates
The redemption rates for SkyWing Miles vary based on the type of redemption and the destination.
- **Domestic Flights (One-Way)**:
    - Economy Class: 10,000 miles.
    - Premium Economy Class: 20,000 miles.
    - First Class: 30,000 miles.
- **International Flights (One-Way)**:
    - Economy Class: 25,000 miles.
    - Premium Economy Class: 50,000 miles.
    - Business Class: 75,000 miles.
    - First Class: 100,000 miles.
- **Seat Upgrades (One-Way)**:
    - Domestic Economy to Premium Economy: 5,000 miles.
    - Domestic Economy to First Class: 15,000 miles.
    - International Economy to Premium Economy: 10,000 miles.
    - International Economy to Business Class: 25,000 miles.
    - International Economy to First Class: 40,000 miles.
- **Hotel Stays**:
    - 5,000 miles per night (depending on the hotel category).
- **Car Rentals**:
    - 2,500 miles per day (depending on the car category).

## Miles Expiration Policy
SkyWing Miles do not expire as long as there is at least one qualifying activity on the account every 24 months. A qualifying activity is defined as any activity that earns or redeems miles.
- **Qualifying Activity**:
    - Earning miles on a SkyWing flight.
    - Earning miles on a partner airline flight.
    - Earning miles on a SkyWing Visa Signature credit card purchase.
    - Redeeming miles for a flight or other reward.
- **Inactivity Penalty**: If there is no qualifying activity on an account for 24 months, all miles in the account will be forfeited.

## Miles Reinstatement
Miles that have been forfeited due to inactivity can be reinstated for a fee.
- **Reinstatement Fee**: $50 for every 10,000 miles reinstated.
- **Reinstatement Period**: Miles can be reinstated within 12 months of forfeiture.

## Transferring Miles
SkyWing Miles can be transferred to other SkyWing Rewards members for a fee.
- **Transfer Fee**: $0.01 per mile transferred, plus a $20 transaction fee.
- **Minimum Transfer**: 1,000 miles.
- **Maximum Transfer**: 100,000 miles per calendar year.
