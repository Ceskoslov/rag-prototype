# Credit Card Tier Benefits (Silver, Gold, Platinum)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the tier benefits for the SkyWing Visa Signature card.

## SkyWing Rewards Status and Tier Benefits
The SkyWing Visa Signature card offers various benefits based on the cardholder's SkyWing Rewards status.
- **Silver Status (Basic)**:
    - Earn 5 miles for every $1 spent on SkyWing flights.
    - One free checked bag on domestic SkyWing flights.
    - Priority boarding (Zone 4).
    - Access to the SkyLounge (limited to 1 visit per year).
- **Gold Status (Preferred)**:
    - Earn 7 miles for every $1 spent on SkyWing flights.
    - Two free checked bags on domestic SkyWing flights.
    - Priority boarding (Zone 3).
    - Access to the SkyLounge (limited to 2 visits per year).
    - Anniversary companion certificate (one free companion ticket for a domestic flight).
- **Platinum Status (Elite)**:
    - Earn 10 miles for every $1 spent on SkyWing flights.
    - Three free checked bags on domestic and international SkyWing flights.
    - Priority boarding (Zone 2).
    - Access to the SkyLounge (unlimited visits).
    - First Class upgrades (based on availability).
    - No change or cancellation fees on SkyWing flights.

## Maintaining and Upgrading Status
SkyWing Rewards members can maintain or upgrade their status by earning a certain number of SkyWing Miles and completing a certain number of flights in a calendar year.
- **Silver Status (Basic)**:
    - Earn 25,000 miles and complete 25 flights in a calendar year.
- **Gold Status (Preferred)**:
    - Earn 50,000 miles and complete 50 flights in a calendar year.
- **Platinum Status (Elite)**:
    - Earn 75,000 miles and complete 75 flights in a calendar year.

## Anniversary Companion Certificate
The anniversary companion certificate is an annual benefit for Gold and Platinum members.
- **Expiration**: The companion certificate expires 12 months from the date it is issued.
- **Blackout Dates**: The companion certificate is not valid during certain blackout dates (e.g., major holidays).
- **Seat Availability**: The companion certificate is subject to seat availability on the flight.
- **Companion Fare**: The companion ticket is free, but the companion must pay all applicable taxes and fees.

## SkyLounge Access
Access to the SkyLounge is a premium benefit for SkyWing Rewards members.
- **Lounge Amenities**: The SkyLounge offers various amenities, including:
    - Complimentary food and beverages.
    - High-speed WiFi.
    - Comfortable seating and workspace.
    - Showers and spa services (at select locations).
- **Lounge Locations**: SkyLounges are located in major airports served by SkyWing.

## First Class Upgrades
First Class upgrades are available for Platinum members on a first-come, first-served basis.
- **Upgrade Process**: Platinum members can request an upgrade at the time of booking or at the airport check-in counter.
- **Upgrade Confirmation**: Upgrades will be confirmed 24-48 hours before the scheduled departure time, based on seat availability.
