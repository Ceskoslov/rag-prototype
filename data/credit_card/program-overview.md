# SkyWing Visa Signature Co-branded Credit Card Program Overview

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with Horizon Bank to offer the SkyWing Visa Signature credit card, a co-branded program designed to reward our most loyal passengers. This document provides an overview of the program's key features, benefits, and eligibility criteria.

## SkyWing Visa Signature Card Options
The program offers two levels of the SkyWing Visa Signature card:
- **SkyWing Explorer Card**: The standard card level, designed for occasional travelers.
- **SkyWing Premier Card**: The premium card level, offering enhanced benefits and higher rewards for frequent flyers.

## Miles Earning and Redemption
SkyWing Visa Signature cardholders earn "SkyWing Miles" on all purchases.
- **SkyWing Purchases**: Earn 2 miles for every $1 spent on SkyWing tickets and services.
- **Dining and Grocery Purchases**: Earn 1.5 miles for every $1 spent on dining and groceries.
- **All Other Purchases**: Earn 1 mile for every $1 spent on all other purchases.
- **SkyWing Miles Redemption**: SkyWing Miles can be redeemed for flights, seat upgrades, hotel stays, and car rentals.

## Credit Card Tier Benefits
The SkyWing Visa Signature card offers various benefits based on the card level and the cardholder's loyalty status.
- **SkyWing Explorer Card Benefits**:
    - No annual fee for the first year.
    - 20,000 bonus SkyWing Miles after spending $1,000 in the first 3 months.
    - One free checked bag on all SkyWing flights.
    - Priority boarding (Zone 2).
- **SkyWing Premier Card Benefits**:
    - $95 annual fee.
    - 50,000 bonus SkyWing Miles after spending $3,000 in the first 3 months.
    - Two free checked bags on all SkyWing flights.
    - Priority boarding (Zone 1).
    - Anniversary companion certificate (one free companion ticket for a domestic flight).
    - Access to the SkyLounge (limited to 2 visits per year).

## Annual Fee Waiver Conditions
The annual fee for the SkyWing Premier Card may be waived in certain circumstances.
- **SkyWing Rewards Status**: The annual fee is waived for SkyWing Rewards Gold, Platinum, and Diamond members.
- **Spending Threshold**: The annual fee is waived for cardholders who spend at least $30,000 on their SkyWing Premier Card in the preceding calendar year.

## Fraud Detection and Dispute Resolution
Horizon Bank, the issuer of the SkyWing Visa Signature card, provides 24/7 fraud monitoring and dispute resolution services.
- **Fraud Monitoring**: Horizon Bank uses advanced technology to detect and prevent unauthorized transactions on SkyWing Visa Signature cards.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Cardholders can dispute unauthorized or incorrect transactions through the Horizon Bank online portal or by calling the customer service number on the back of their card.

## Card Application and Approval Criteria
Customers can apply for the SkyWing Visa Signature card online at `skywing.com/credit-card` or at any SkyWing check-in counter.
- **Eligibility**: Applicants must be at least 18 years old and have a valid Social Security number or Taxpayer Identification Number.
- **Credit Score**: Approval is subject to creditworthiness and other factors. A minimum credit score of 670 is generally required for the SkyWing Explorer Card and 720 for the SkyWing Premier Card.
- **Income**: Applicants must have a steady source of income.
