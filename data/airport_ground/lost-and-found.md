# Lost and Found Procedures

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to helping its passengers locate and recover their lost items. This document outlines SkyWing's lost and found procedures at the airport.

## Reporting a Lost Item
If you have lost an item on a SkyWing flight or at a SkyWing gate, you should report it as soon as possible.
- **Lost Item Report**: Submit a lost item report via the SkyWing online lost and found portal at `skywing.com/lost-and-found`.
- **In-Person Report**: You can also report a lost item in person at any SkyWing check-in counter or at the airport's lost and found office.
- **Required Information**:
    - Your name and contact information.
    - Booking reference (PNR).
    - Flight number and date.
    - Description of the lost item.
    - Where and when you lost the item.

## Item Recovery and Retrieval
If your lost item is located, SkyWing will notify you and provide instructions for its retrieval.
- **Notification**: SkyWing will notify you via email or phone if your lost item is found.
- **Retrieval Options**:
    - **In-Person Retrieval**: You can retrieve your lost item in person at the airport's lost and found office.
    - **Shipping Retrieval**: SkyWing can ship your lost item to your local address for a fee.
- **Identification Verification**: You must present a valid government-issued ID to retrieve your lost item.

## Lost and Found Storage Period
SkyWing will store lost items for a period of 30 days.
- **Storage Location**: Lost items will be stored at the airport's lost and found office.
- **Disposal**: Any items that are not retrieved within 30 days will be disposed of or donated to charity.

## Items Not Found
If your lost item is not found within 30 days, SkyWing will close your lost item report.
- **Final Notification**: SkyWing will send you a final notification if your lost item is not found.
- **Liability**: SkyWing is not liable for lost items that are not found.

## High-Value Items
For high-value items (e.g., jewelry, electronics, cash), SkyWing will take additional security measures to ensure their safe retrieval.
- **Secure Storage**: High-value items will be stored in a secure location at the airport's lost and found office.
- **Enhanced Verification**: SkyWing will require additional verification before releasing a high-value item.

## Items Lost at Other Airport Locations
If you have lost an item at other airport locations (e.g., security checkpoints, restaurants, shops), you should contact the airport's lost and found office or the relevant airport authority.
- **Airport Lost and Found Office**: Call the airport's main lost and found office (available 24/7).
- **TSA Lost and Found**: If you lost an item at a security checkpoint, contact the Transportation Security Administration (TSA).
