# Gate Assignment and Boarding Procedures

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to providing a safe and efficient boarding experience for all our passengers. This document outlines SkyWing's gate assignment and boarding procedures for all its flights.

## Gate Assignment
SkyWing gates are assigned by the airport authority and may change without notice.
- **Gate Assignment Notification**: SkyWing will notify passengers of their gate assignment via the airport's flight information displays, the SkyWing website, the mobile app, and at the gate.
- **Gate Information Updates**: SkyWing will update passengers on any gate changes via the same methods.
- **Gate Arrival**: Passengers are asked to arrive at the gate at least 30 minutes before the scheduled departure time.

## Boarding Process
SkyWing uses a zone-based boarding process to ensure a smooth and efficient boarding experience.
- **Zone 1 (Premium)**:
    - First Class passengers.
    - SkyWing Rewards Platinum members.
- **Zone 2 (Priority)**:
    - Business Class passengers.
    - SkyWing Rewards Gold members.
    - SkyWing Visa Signature Premier cardholders.
- **Zone 3 (Preferred)**:
    - Premium Economy Class passengers.
    - SkyWing Rewards Silver members.
    - Families with young children (under 2 years old).
- **Zone 4 (Standard)**:
    - Economy Class passengers (rows 1-15).
- **Zone 5 (Standard)**:
    - Economy Class passengers (rows 16-30).
- **Zone 6 (Standard)**:
    - Economy Class passengers (rows 31+).

## Boarding Requirements
A valid boarding pass (digital or paper) and government-issued ID are required for boarding.
- **Boarding Pass Verification**: SkyWing representatives at the gate will verify each passenger's boarding pass.
- **ID Verification**: SkyWing representatives will also verify each passenger's government-issued ID (e.g., driver's license, passport).
- **Security Check**: Passengers may be subject to additional security checks at the gate.

## Boarding Deadlines
Passengers must be at the boarding gate at least 30 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
- **Gate Closing**: The boarding gate will close 15 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
- **Failure to Comply**: SkyWing reserves the right to deny boarding to any passenger who arrives at the gate after it has closed.

## Special Assistance Boarding
SkyWing provides priority boarding for passengers who require special assistance (e.g., wheelchair assistance, unaccompanied minors).
- **Priority Boarding**: Special assistance passengers will be boarded before all other zones.
- **Assistance Services**: SkyWing representatives will assist special assistance passengers with their boarding.

## Onboard Boarding
SkyWing's flight attendants will assist passengers on board the aircraft with their seat, luggage, and other needs.
- **Overhead Bin Space**: Flight attendants will help passengers find space in the overhead bins for their carry-on baggage.
- **Seat Assignment**: Flight attendants will verify each passenger's seat assignment and assist them with their seat.
