# Lounge Access Policies and Amenities

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) provides premium airport lounge services for its passengers at major airports worldwide. This document outlines SkyWing's lounge access policies and amenities for its SkyLounges.

## SkyLounge Access Policies
Access to the SkyLounge is available to the following categories of passengers:
- **First Class Passengers**: Complimentary access for First Class passengers on domestic and international flights.
- **Business Class Passengers**: Complimentary access for Business Class passengers on international flights.
- **SkyWing Rewards Platinum Members**: Unlimited complimentary access for Platinum members and one guest.
- **SkyWing Rewards Gold Members**: Two complimentary visits per calendar year for Gold members and their guests.
- **SkyWing Rewards Silver Members**: One complimentary visit per calendar year for Silver members.
- **SkyWing Visa Signature Premier Cardholders**: Two complimentary visits per calendar year for cardholders.
- **SkyWing Lounge Day Pass**: A $50 USD day pass can be purchased at the lounge entrance for other passengers, subject to availability.

## SkyLounge Amenities
SkyLounges offer various amenities to provide a comfortable and productive environment for passengers:
- **Food and Beverages**: A variety of complimentary snacks, meals, and beverages, including hot and cold options, are available.
- **Bar Service**: A full-service bar offering a selection of beer, wine, and spirits is available (at select locations).
- **High-Speed WiFi**: Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access is available throughout the lounge.
- **Business Center**: A dedicated workspace with computers, printers, and charging stations is available.
- **Comfortable Seating**: A variety of comfortable seating options, including armchairs, tables, and recliners, are available.
- **Showers and Spa Services**: Showers and spa services (e.g., massages) are available at select SkyLounge locations.
- **Children's Play Area**: A dedicated play area for children is available at some locations.
- **Flight Information**: Real-time flight information is displayed on screens throughout the lounge.

## SkyLounge Locations
SkyLounges are located in the following airports served by SkyWing:
- **North America**: New York (JFK), Los Angeles (LAX), Chicago (ORD), San Francisco (SFO), Miami (MIA), Toronto (YYZ).
- **Europe**: London (LHR), Paris (CDG), Frankfurt (FRA), Amsterdam (AMS), Madrid (MAD), Rome (FCO).
- **Asia-Pacific**: Tokyo (NRT/HND), Hong Kong (HKG), Singapore (SIN), Seoul (ICN), Sydney (SYD), Auckland (AKL).
- **Middle East and Africa**: Dubai (DXB), Abu Dhabi (AUH), Johannesburg (JNB).

## SkyLounge Rules and Etiquette
To ensure a pleasant experience for all lounge guests, the following rules and etiquette apply:
- **Dress Code**: Smart casual dress is required for all lounge guests.
- **Electronic Devices**: Guests are asked to use headphones when using electronic devices and to keep volume at a reasonable level.
- **Children**: Children must be supervised by an adult at all times.
- **Smoking**: Smoking (including e-cigarettes) is strictly prohibited in all SkyLounges.
- **Lounge Capacity**: SkyWing reserves the right to limit lounge access if the lounge is at capacity.
