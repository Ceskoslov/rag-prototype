# Flight Delay and Cancellation Handling Procedures

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to providing a safe and reliable travel experience for all our passengers. However, flight disruptions can occur, and this document outlines SkyWing's procedures for handling flight delays and cancellations at the airport.

## Flight Delay Procedures
In the event of a flight delay, SkyWing will provide passengers with real-time updates and assistance.
- **Flight Information**: SkyWing will provide passengers with updates on the flight's status via the airport's flight information displays, the SkyWing website, the mobile app, and at the gate.
- **Assistance Services**: SkyWing representatives at the gate will provide assistance to passengers, including re-booking and providing refreshments.
- **Schedules and Delays**: If a flight is delayed more than 2 hours, SkyWing will provide passengers with a $15 USD meal voucher.
- **Schedules and Delays (Domestic)**: If a domestic flight is delayed more than 4 hours, SkyWing will provide passengers with a $25 USD meal voucher and a 20% discount on a future flight.
- **Schedules and Delays (International)**: If an international flight is delayed more than 4 hours, SkyWing will provide passengers with a $35 USD meal voucher and a 30% discount on a future flight.

## Flight Cancellation Procedures
In the event of a flight cancellation, SkyWing will provide passengers with re-booking and assistance.
- **Flight Cancellation**: If a flight is cancelled, SkyWing will notify passengers as soon as possible via email, text message, or at the gate.
- **Re-booking**: SkyWing will re-book passengers on the next available flight to their final destination at no additional cost.
- **Assistance Services**: SkyWing representatives at the gate will provide assistance to passengers, including re-booking and providing refreshments.
- **Schedules and Cancellations (Domestic)**: If a domestic flight is cancelled, SkyWing will provide passengers with a $50 USD meal voucher and a 50% discount on a future flight.
- **Schedules and Cancellations (International)**: If an international flight is cancelled, SkyWing will provide passengers with a $75 USD meal voucher and a 75% discount on a future flight.

## Overnight Accommodation
If a flight delay or cancellation requires an overnight stay, SkyWing will provide passengers with hotel accommodation and transport.
- **Hotel Accommodation**: SkyWing will provide passengers with a hotel room for one night, subject to availability.
- **Transport**: SkyWing will provide passengers with transport between the airport and the hotel.
- **Breakfast and Dinner**: SkyWing will provide passengers with breakfast and dinner at the hotel.

## Passenger Rights and Compensation (EU261)
For flights departing from or arriving at an airport within the European Union (EU), SkyWing will provide passengers with assistance and compensation in accordance with EU Regulation 261/2004 (EU261).
- **Fixed-Sum Compensation**: Under EU261, passengers may be eligible for fixed-sum compensation (ranging from EUR 250 to EUR 600) for flight cancellations and significant delays.
- **Right to Care**: Under EU261, passengers are entitled to food, refreshments, and communication for flight delays and cancellations.

## Extraordinary Circumstances
SkyWing is not required to pay fixed-sum compensation under EU261 if the flight disruption was caused by "extraordinary circumstances" (e.g., extreme weather, air traffic control strikes, security threats).
