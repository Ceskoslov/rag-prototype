# Ground Transportation Partnerships

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) has partnered with various ground transportation providers to offer its passengers convenient and reliable transportation to and from the airport. This document outlines SkyWing's ground transportation partnerships and the benefits available to SkyWing passengers.

## Rideshare Partnerships (Uber, Lyft)
SkyWing has partnered with Uber and Lyft to offer SkyWing Rewards members exclusive benefits and discounts.
- **SkyWing Rewards Benefits**:
    - Earn 2 SkyWing Miles for every $1 spent on Uber or Lyft rides to and from the airport.
    - 10% discount on Uber or Lyft rides for SkyWing Rewards Platinum members.
    - Priority pickup for SkyWing Rewards Platinum members at select airports.
- **Linking Your Accounts**: To earn SkyWing Miles, you must link your SkyWing Rewards account to your Uber or Lyft account.

## Car Rental Partnerships (Hertz, Avis, Enterprise)
SkyWing has partnered with Hertz, Avis, and Enterprise to offer SkyWing Rewards members special rates and benefits.
- **SkyWing Rewards Benefits**:
    - Up to 25% discount on car rentals with Hertz, Avis, or Enterprise.
    - Earn 500 SkyWing Miles for every car rental.
    - Complimentary car upgrades for SkyWing Rewards Platinum and Gold members (based on availability).
- **Booking Your Car Rental**: To receive these benefits, you must book your car rental through the SkyWing website or mobile app.

## Public Transportation Partnerships
SkyWing has partnered with public transportation authorities at major airports to offer its passengers discounted fares and priority access.
- **Airport Rail Link**: SkyWing Rewards members receive a 10% discount on airport rail link fares at select airports (e.g., London Heathrow Express, Tokyo Narita Express).
- **Airport Express Bus**: SkyWing Rewards members also receive a 10% discount on airport express bus fares at select airports.
- **Priority Access**: SkyWing Rewards Platinum and Gold members receive priority access to public transportation boarding areas at select airports.

## Hotel Shuttle Partnerships
SkyWing has partnered with major hotel chains to offer its passengers complimentary or discounted shuttle service to and from the airport.
- **Complimentary Shuttle**: Many SkyWing-partner hotels offer complimentary shuttle service for SkyWing passengers.
- **Discounted Shuttle**: Other SkyWing-partner hotels offer discounted shuttle service for SkyWing passengers.
- **Booking Your Hotel Shuttle**: To receive these benefits, you must book your hotel stay through the SkyWing website or mobile app and request the shuttle service at the time of booking.

## Chauffeur Service (SkyWing Premier)
SkyWing offers a complimentary chauffeur service for its First Class and Business Class passengers on select international flights.
- **Complimentary Chauffeur Service**: SkyWing Premier Chauffeur service is available for First Class and Business Class passengers traveling between the airport and their home or hotel.
- **Service Area**: The chauffeur service is available within a 50-mile radius of the airport.
- **Booking Your Chauffeur Service**: To receive this service, you must book your flight at least 48 hours in advance and request the chauffeur service at the time of booking.
