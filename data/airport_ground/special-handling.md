# Special Handling (Wheelchairs, Pets, Unaccompanied Minors)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to providing a safe and comfortable travel experience for all our passengers, including those with special needs or requirements. This document outlines SkyWing's special handling procedures for wheelchairs, pets, and unaccompanied minors.

## Wheelchair Assistance
SkyWing provides wheelchair assistance to passengers who have difficulty walking or need assistance navigating the airport.
- **Requesting Assistance**: Passengers can request wheelchair assistance at the time of booking or at the airport check-in counter.
- **Types of Assistance**: SkyWing provides three types of wheelchair assistance:
    - **WCHR (Wheelchair-Ramp)**: For passengers who can walk but have difficulty walking long distances.
    - **WCHS (Wheelchair-Steps)**: For passengers who can walk on level ground but cannot navigate steps.
    - **WCHC (Wheelchair-Cabin)**: For passengers who are completely immobile and need assistance in and out of their seat and to and from the lavatory.
- **Assistance Services**: SkyWing representatives will assist passengers with their luggage, check-in, security, and boarding.
- **Onboard Assistance**: SkyWing's flight attendants will assist passengers on board the aircraft with their seat, meals, and lavatory.

## Traveling with Pets
SkyWing allows passengers to travel with small cats and dogs in the cabin on domestic and international flights.
- **Pet Carrier**: Pets must be transported in an airline-approved pet carrier that fits under the seat in front of the passenger.
- **Carrier Dimensions**: Maximum carrier dimensions are 45cm x 30cm x 25cm (18in x 12in x 10in).
- **Pet Weight**: The combined weight of the pet and its carrier must not exceed 9kg/20lbs.
- **Fees**: A $125 USD fee applies to each pet in the cabin per flight.
- **Documentation**: For international flights, pets must have all necessary health and vaccination certificates.
- **Service Animals**: Service animals (e.g., guide dogs) are allowed in the cabin free of charge and do not count toward the pet limit.

## Unaccompanied Minors (UM)
SkyWing provides a special handling service for unaccompanied minors (UMs) traveling alone.
- **Age Requirement**: Children between the ages of 5 and 14 traveling alone are considered unaccompanied minors.
- **UM Service Fee**: A $150 USD UM service fee applies per child per flight.
- **Booking Process**: UM bookings must be made through SkyWing's customer service or at an airport check-in counter.
- **Airport Procedures**: UMs must be checked in at the airport counter by a parent or legal guardian. The parent or legal guardian must remain at the airport until the flight has departed.
- **Onboard Care**: SkyWing's flight attendants will supervise and assist UMs during the flight.
- **Arrival Procedures**: UMs will be met at the arrival gate by a SkyWing representative and released only to the designated adult specified on the UM form.

## Other Special Handling Requirements
SkyWing also provides special handling services for passengers with other needs, including:
- **Oxygen**: SkyWing allows passengers to use their own portable oxygen concentrators (POCs) on board the aircraft.
- **Medical Equipment**: Passengers can transport medical equipment (e.g., CPAP machines) in the cabin or as checked baggage.
- **Stretchers**: Stretchers are available on select international flights for a fee and must be requested at least 48 hours in advance.
