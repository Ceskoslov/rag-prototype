# Baggage Weight Limits and Excess Fees (Domestic/International)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) is committed to handling passenger baggage with care and in accordance with international and regional regulations. This document outlines SkyWing's baggage weight limits and excess fees for domestic and international flights.

## Domestic Baggage Weight Limits and Fees
For domestic flights within the United States, SkyWing's baggage weight limits and fees are as follows:
- **Economy Class**:
    - One free checked bag (up to 23kg/50lbs).
    - Second checked bag: $40 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
    - Third checked bag: $100 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
- **Premium Economy Class**:
    - Two free checked bags (up to 23kg/50lbs each).
    - Third checked bag: $100 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
- **First Class**:
    - Three free checked bags (up to 32kg/70lbs each).

## International Baggage Weight Limits and Fees
For international flights, SkyWing's baggage weight limits and fees vary by destination:
- **Economy Class (International)**:
    - One free checked bag (up to 23kg/50lbs).
    - Second checked bag: $100 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
    - Third checked bag: $200 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
- **Premium Economy Class (International)**:
    - Two free checked bags (up to 23kg/50lbs each).
    - Third checked bag: $200 USD (up to 23kg/50lbs).
- **Business Class (International)**:
    - Two free checked bags (up to 32kg/70lbs each).
    - Third checked bag: $200 USD (up to 32kg/70lbs).
- **First Class (International)**:
    - Three free checked bags (up to 32kg/70lbs each).

## Excess Baggage Weight and Size Fees
Baggage that exceeds the weight or size limits will be subject to excess baggage fees.
- **Overweight Fee (24kg-32kg / 51lbs-70lbs)**: $100 USD per bag.
- **Overweight Fee (Over 32kg / 70lbs)**: Bags over 32kg/70lbs will not be accepted as checked baggage and must be shipped via SkyWing Cargo.
- **Oversized Fee (Over 158cm / 62in linear dimensions)**: $200 USD per bag.
- **Maximum Dimensions**: Bags that exceed 203cm/80in linear dimensions will not be accepted as checked baggage.

## SkyWing Rewards Status and Baggage Benefits
SkyWing Rewards members may be eligible for additional baggage benefits based on their status.
- **Silver Status (Basic)**: One additional free checked bag on domestic flights.
- **Gold Status (Preferred)**: Two additional free checked bags on domestic flights and one additional free checked bag on international flights.
- **Platinum Status (Elite)**: Three additional free checked bags on all flights.

## Carry-on Baggage
All passengers are allowed one piece of carry-on baggage and one personal item (e.g., purse, laptop bag).
- **Carry-on Baggage Dimensions**: Maximum dimensions are 56cm x 36cm x 23cm (22in x 14in x 9in).
- **Personal Item Dimensions**: Maximum dimensions are 45cm x 35cm x 20cm (18in x 14in x 8in).
- **Weight Limit**: Carry-on baggage must not exceed 10kg/22lbs.
