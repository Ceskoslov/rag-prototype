# Check-in Procedures (Online, Kiosk, Counter)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines (SkyWing) provides passengers with multiple check-in options to ensure a smooth and efficient start to their journey. This document outlines the check-in procedures for online, kiosk, and counter check-in.

## Online Check-in
Online check-in is the most convenient option for most passengers and is available for all SkyWing flights.
- **Availability**: Online check-in is available from 24 hours to 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time for domestic flights and 24 hours to 90 minutes for international flights.
- **Process**: Passengers can check in online through the SkyWing website at `skywing.com/check-in` or through the SkyWing mobile app.
- **Boarding Pass**: After completing online check-in, passengers can download or print their boarding pass or save it to their mobile device.
- **Baggage**: Passengers who have checked baggage can drop it off at the designated baggage drop-off counter at the airport.

## Kiosk Check-in
Self-service kiosks are available at most major airports served by SkyWing and provide a quick and easy way to check in.
- **Availability**: Kiosk check-in is available from 3 hours to 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time for domestic flights and 4 hours to 90 minutes for international flights.
- **Process**: Passengers can use the self-service kiosks at the airport to check in, select their seat, and print their boarding pass and baggage tags.
- **Identification**: Passengers must have their booking reference (PNR) or their government-issued ID to use the kiosk.
- **Baggage**: After printing their baggage tags at the kiosk, passengers can attach them to their bags and drop them off at the designated baggage drop-off counter.

## Counter Check-in
Counter check-in is available for all SkyWing flights and is mandatory for certain types of travel (e.g., unaccompanied minors, passengers traveling with pets).
- **Availability**: Counter check-in is available from 3 hours to 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time for domestic flights and 4 hours to 90 minutes for international flights.
- **Process**: Passengers can check in with a SkyWing representative at the airport check-in counter.
- **Identification**: Passengers must present a valid government-issued ID for domestic flights and a valid passport for international flights.
- **Special Assistance**: Passengers who require special assistance (e.g., wheelchair assistance) should notify the SkyWing representative at the check-in counter.

## Check-in Deadlines
Passengers must complete check-in by the following deadlines:
- **Domestic Flights**: 60 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
- **International Flights**: 90 minutes before the scheduled departure time.
- **Failure to Comply**: SkyWing reserves the right to cancel a passenger's reservation or deny boarding if they fail to comply with these deadlines.

## Boarding Passes
A valid boarding pass (digital or paper) is required for boarding.
- **Digital Boarding Pass**: Digital boarding passes are available for most passengers who check in online or through the mobile app.
- **Paper Boarding Pass**: Paper boarding passes can be printed at a self-service kiosk or at the airport check-in counter.
