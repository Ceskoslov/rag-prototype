# Laptop Provisioning and Return Policy

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines provides employees with corporate-issued computing equipment to ensure a secure and productive work environment. This policy outlines the procedures for receiving, maintaining, and returning laptops and associated peripherals.

## Standard Issue Equipment
The standard SkyWing employee kit includes:
- **Primary Device**: 14" Business-grade laptop (Dell Latitude or MacBook Pro, based on role requirements).
- **Peripherals**: 1x Universal USB-C Docking Station, 1x Wireless Mouse, 1x Wireless Keyboard, and 2x 24" External Monitors.
- **Accessories**: 1x Laptop Power Adapter and 1x Protective Carrying Case.

## Laptop Provisioning (New Hires)
1. **Asset Allocation**: Managers must submit an "Employee Onboarding Request" (IT Form 102) via the SkyWing HR portal at least 14 days prior to the start date.
2. **Configuration**: All laptops are pre-loaded with the "SkyWing Base Image" (Windows 11 Enterprise or macOS 14 Sonoma) and essential security software.
3. **Delivery**: Equipment is shipped directly to the employee's designated primary office location or home address (for remote employees).
4. **Initial Login**: New hires must connect to the corporate network or VPN to perform the initial login and profile synchronization.

## Maintenance and Upgrades
- **Service Life**: Laptops are replaced on a 36-month refresh cycle. IT will notify employees 60 days before their scheduled upgrade.
- **Physical Damage**: Employees are responsible for the physical security of their issued equipment. Accidents must be reported via the IT Help Desk portal within 24 hours.
- **Repair Procedures**: If a device requires repair, a "Loaner Unit" will be provided while the original device is being serviced.

## Software and Security
- **Restricted Access**: Administrative rights are not granted by default. Software must be installed via the "SkyWing App Store" (Company Portal).
- **Encryption**: All SkyWing laptops utilize full-disk encryption (BitLocker for Windows, FileVault for macOS). Encryption must not be disabled.
- **Compliance Checks**: Devices that have not connected to the SkyWing network for more than 45 days will be automatically disabled for security reasons.

## Laptop Return (Offboarding)
Upon resignation, termination, or retirement, all issued equipment must be returned to the IT Asset Management team.
- **Timeline**: Equipment must be returned within 3 business days of the last day of employment.
- **Condition**: Laptops must be returned in good working condition, free of stickers, personal decals, or permanent markings.
- **Shipping (Remote Employees)**: IT will provide a pre-paid, insured shipping label and box for equipment returns.
- **Financial Liability**: Failure to return equipment within 30 days will result in a payroll deduction or an invoice for the current fair market value of the assets.

## Inventory Audits
- **Annual Verification**: Once per calendar year, employees must complete a digital asset verification via the IT Asset Portal.
- **Verification Steps**: Confirm the Serial Number (S/N) and Asset Tag number on the bottom of the device matches the record on file.

## Contact Information
For questions regarding equipment or to request an early refresh due to performance issues, please contact the IT Asset Team at `assets@skywing.com`.
