# IT Security Policies (2FA, Phishing Awareness)

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines is committed to protecting the privacy and security of our passengers and employees. This document outlines the mandatory security policies for all SkyWing employees, contractors, and vendors.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) / Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
SkyWing Airlines requires 2FA for all corporate resources, including:
- **VPN Access** (SkyConnect VPN)
- **Corporate Email** (Microsoft 365 Outlook)
- **HR and Payroll Systems** (SkyWing People Portal)
- **Internal Tools and Databases** (SkyWing Ops, Finance, Legal)

### Required Authenticator App
All employees must install the "SkyWing Authenticator" app on their company-issued or personal mobile device.
1. **Setup**: Follow the instructions at `sso.skywing.com/2fa` to link your device to your SSO account.
2. **Push Notifications**: When logging into a protected system, you will receive a push notification on your mobile device.
3. **Verification**: Confirm the login request by clicking "Approve" and entering your device's passcode or using biometric authentication (FaceID or Fingerprint).

### Backup 2FA Options
In the event that your primary device is unavailable, you can use:
- **Hardware Token**: A YubiKey or similar security key can be requested from the IT Security team.
- **Temporary Bypass Code**: Valid for 24 hours, available via the IT Help Desk after identity verification.

## Phishing Awareness and Reporting
Phishing is a common social engineering tactic used to gain unauthorized access to SkyWing systems.

### Red Flags of Phishing
- **Sense of Urgency**: Emails that demand immediate action (e.g., "Your account will be suspended in 2 hours!").
- **Generic Greetings**: Use of generic terms like "Dear Valued Employee" instead of your name.
- **Suspicious Links**: Hover over links to verify the destination URL (e.g., `skywing.com.fake-site.net`).
- **Requests for Sensitive Information**: SkyWing IT will never ask for your password via email.
- **Unusual Sender Address**: Check the sender's email address for slight misspellings (e.g., `admin@skywiing.com`).

### Reporting a Suspected Phishing Email
1. **Do Not Click**: Do not click on any links or open any attachments in the suspicious email.
2. **Report Message**: Use the "Report Message" button in the Outlook toolbar and select "Phishing".
3. **Direct Email**: Alternatively, you can forward the suspicious email as an attachment to `phish-report@skywing.com`.

## Phishing Simulation Program
SkyWing IT Security conducts monthly phishing simulations to assess and improve employee awareness.
- **Purpose**: To provide realistic training and identify areas for further security education.
- **Consequences**: Employees who repeatedly fail the phishing simulation (more than twice in 6 months) will be required to complete a mandatory 1-hour security awareness training module.

## Data Protection and Confidentiality
- **Clear Desk Policy**: Sensitive documents must be stored in locked cabinets when not in use.
- **Screen Locking**: Employees must lock their computer screens (Windows+L or Control+Command+Q) when leaving their workstation.
- **Removable Media**: The use of unauthorized USB drives, external hard drives, or SD cards is strictly prohibited on SkyWing corporate devices.
- **Personal Email**: Use of personal email (e.g., Gmail, Yahoo) for SkyWing business or the transmission of SkyWing data is not permitted.

## Incident Reporting
If you believe your account has been compromised or you witness a security breach, contact the Security Operations Center (SOC) immediately.
- **SOC Hotline**: Extension 9111 (available 24/7/365)
- **Email**: `soc@skywing.com`
- **Location**: SkyWing Hub Office, Building B, 4th Floor

## Compliance
Failure to comply with these security policies may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment.
