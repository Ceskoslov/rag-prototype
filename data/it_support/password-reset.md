# Password Reset Procedures

## Overview
SkyWing Airlines uses a unified Single Sign-On (SSO) system for managing access to all corporate resources, including email, VPN, and internal tools. This document provides instructions for resetting passwords and managing security credentials.

## Password Requirements
All SkyWing accounts must adhere to the following complexity requirements:
- **Length**: Minimum 14 characters.
- **Characters**: Must contain at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one numeric digit, and one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $, %).
- **History**: Cannot reuse any of the previous 12 passwords.
- **Expiration**: Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Users receive an automated email notification 14, 7, and 1 day before expiration.

## Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)
Employees are encouraged to use the automated SkyWing SSPR portal at `sso.skywing.com/reset`.
1. **Verification**: Enter your Employee ID and your corporate email address.
2. **Identity Proof**: Complete two of the three pre-configured security questions OR enter the 6-digit verification code sent to your registered mobile device.
3. **New Password**: Enter and confirm your new password according to the requirements listed above.

## Manual Reset via IT Help Desk
If you are locked out or cannot access the SSPR portal, you must contact the IT Help Desk via phone (extension 5555).
- **Identity Verification**: For security purposes, you will be asked to provide your Employee ID, date of hire, and the last four digits of your registered personal phone number.
- **Temporary Password**: Upon verification, a 24-hour temporary password will be issued. 
- **Required Change**: You must change this temporary password during your next login attempt.

## VPN and Mobile Device Syncing
After changing your SSO password, you must manually update the saved credentials on:
- **SkyConnect VPN Client**: Disconnect and reconnect using the new password.
- **Microsoft Outlook (Mobile)**: The app will prompt for the new password within 15 minutes of the change.
- **Employee WiFi**: "SkyWing-Staff" WiFi will require you to forget the network and reconnect with the new credentials.

## Account Lockout Policy
- **Threshold**: Five consecutive failed login attempts will result in an automated 60-minute account lockout.
- **Auto-Unlock**: After 60 minutes, the account will automatically unlock.
- **Emergency Unlock**: Managers can request an emergency unlock for their direct reports via the IT Manager Portal if there is a critical business need.

## Reporting Suspicious Activity
If you receive a password reset notification that you did not initiate, please report it immediately to the Security Operations Center at `soc@skywing.com` or call the IT Security Hotline at extension 9111.
